Dual defect

A general tangent hyperplane is tangent along $\mathbb P^\delta$, where $\delta=\operatorname{codim}(X^\vee)-1$. The defective cases with nonempty dual are $\Gr(2,2m+1)$ for $m\geq2$ ($\delta=2$) and the spinor tenfold ($\delta=4$). The self-dual cases are quadrics, $\Gr(2,5)$ and the spinor tenfold. {{ macros.cite("tevelev", "Theorems 1.12, 9.19 and 10.22; Examples 2.20–2.21 (arXiv version)") }}

Degrees

Computed with Oscar's IntersectionTheory from the first jet bundle: \begin{equation} \deg X^\vee=\int_X c_{d-\delta}\bigl(J_1(\mathcal O_X(1))\bigr)H^\delta, \qquad d=\dim X,\quad H=c_1(\mathcal O_X(1)). \end{equation} {{ macros.cite("tevelev", "Theorems 7.1–7.2 (arXiv version)") }}
