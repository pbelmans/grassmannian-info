Projective duality

For each Grassmannian $X\subset\mathbb P^N$, the table gives the codimension and degree of $X^\vee\subset(\mathbb P^N)^\vee$, and whether $X$ and $X^\vee$ are projectively isomorphic. The embedding is given by the ample generator of $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$.

The dual defect is $\delta=\operatorname{codim}(X^\vee)-1$. A general tangent hyperplane is tangent along a linear space $\mathbb P^\delta$. Among the Grassmannians with nonempty dual, the only defective cases are $\Gr(2,2m+1)$ for $m\geq2$, with $\delta=2$, and the spinor tenfold, with $\delta=4$. The self-dual cases are quadrics, $\Gr(2,5)$ and the spinor tenfold. {{ macros.cite("tevelev", "Theorems 1.12, 9.19 and 10.22; Examples 2.20–2.21 (arXiv version)") }}

The tables cover Dynkin types of rank at most 8. An empty dual has no codimension or degree listed; “not recorded” means that a degree has not yet been entered here. For a self-dual variety, the degree is that of the Grassmannian itself.
