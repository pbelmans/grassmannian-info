References

The literature behind the exceptional collections, quantum cohomology, Lefschetz collections, homological projective duality, and higher Fano properties recorded on this site. Individual Grassmannian pages cite the results that apply to them. Published versions are linked where available; arXiv links are retained for access.

The bibliography is chronological, using publication years for published work and first submission years for preprints. Last updated on 28 October 2026.

Exceptional collections. The constructions of Beilinson and Kapranov were followed by explicit collections in other types. Samokhin–van der Kallen now give full exceptional collections for every split flag variety. This existence statement does not imply that every previously proposed collection is full, rectangular, or Lefschetz. {{ macros.cite("samokhin-vdk") }}

Quantum cohomology. Big quantum cohomology is generically semisimple for every $G/P$. {{ macros.cite("big-qh") }} For small quantum cohomology, the index and Betti numbers give non-semisimplicity obstructions. {{ macros.cite("graded-qh", "Section 2.3") }} Holmes–Muratore settle the remaining exceptional cases: $\mathrm E_7/\mathrm P_2$, $\mathrm E_7/\mathrm P_4$, $\mathrm E_7/\mathrm P_5$, and $\mathrm E_8/\mathrm P_6$ are semisimple at $q=1$. {{ macros.cite("holmes-muratore", "Theorem 1.2") }}
