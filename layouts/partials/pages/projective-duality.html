Dual defect

A general tangent hyperplane is tangent along $\mathbb P^\delta$, where $\delta=\operatorname{codim}(X^\vee)-1$. The defective cases with nonempty dual are $\Gr(2,2m+1)$ for $m\geq2$ ($\delta=2$) and the spinor tenfold ($\delta=4$). The self-dual cases are quadrics, $\Gr(2,5)$ and the spinor tenfold. {{ (partial "components/cite.html" (dict "key" "tevelev" "locator" "Theorems 1.12, 9.19 and 10.22; Examples 2.20–2.21 (arXiv version)")) }}

Degrees
